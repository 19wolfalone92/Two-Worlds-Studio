# TWO WORLDS — Canon v0.1 (production constraints)

**Genre:** sci-fi dark fantasy, mystery, personal drama. **Language on screen:** English.

## Setting
- **Earth:** contemporary technological civilization. Mira (25) investigates her brother Eli's disappearance six months ago.
- **Noctaris:** independent inhabited twilight world, two visible moons, complex societies and a centuries-long war between the Silver Flame Empire and the Black Crown. Neither is uniformly good or evil.
- Unknown lands, ancient beings and lost history complicate the war; the audience gradually learns that both realities branched from a common precursor.

## Central characters
- **Mira**: consciousness born on Earth. Cautious and compassionate. Does **not** acquire military skill by switching bodies.
- **Lyra**: Noctaris-born general of the Black Crown. Same face, different memories and ethics. Her Earth story has equal dramatic weight.
- **Eli**: Mira's younger brother. His consciousness swapped into his Noctaris counterpart **Ilan** six months earlier.
- **Ilan**: Noctaris-born counterpart of Eli; now inhabits Eli's Earth body. His whereabouts and motivations are initially unknown.
- **Taren**: fellow officer, gradually recognizes that Lyra behaves differently and makes a costly choice to help.
- **Varos**: imperial strategist seeking coercive control of the exchange apparatus.
- **Keepers**: fractured organization with partial knowledge of the ancient system.

## Identity invariant — crucial
Mirrors exchange **consciousness, not bodies**. A body stays in its world with its clothing, scars, fitness and biological properties. Memory/personality changes with the occupant. Always track each body and occupant separately in scripts and artwork.

Initial:
Earth Mira-body ← Mira; Noctaris Lyra-body ← Lyra.
After episode 1:
Earth Mira-body ← Lyra; Noctaris Lyra-body ← Mira.
Six months before E01:
Earth Eli-body ← Ilan; Noctaris Ilan-body ← Eli.

## Mirror limits
1. Natural exchange requires two living paired consciousnesses and a temporary activated resonant mirror connection.
2. Pairing is specific. Contact with corresponding surfaces during resonance is required. Mirrors are not general teleportation gates.
3. No objects, armor, armies or extra passengers cross. Physical wounds remain with bodies.
4. Some people have no living matching counterpart and cannot exchange.
5. Mirror systems are fragments of ancient precursor infrastructure; war experiments only accidentally activate the connection, not conveniently every episode.
6. Exchange leaves rare dream/emotion echoes; they cannot transfer arbitrary knowledge on demand.
7. If a counterpart dies, natural reverse exchange for the pair becomes impossible. No resurrection or free body creation.
8. A second transfer needs another real activation under established limits; do not invent a painless emergency return.

## Narrative priorities
- Character decisions lead to consequences, including irreversible loss.
- War has economic and political causes beyond magical spectacle.
- No all-powerful artifact solving every conflict; build setup → payoff ledger.
- Audience does **not** vote on plot branches during launch. This is a scripted storyline.
- Plot inspirations are thematic only; never imitate protected characters, prose or artwork.
